# Attributions

The zodiac background uses astronomical right-ascension/declination geometry and
Western constellation line conventions adapted from the open
[ConstellationLines](https://github.com/MarcvdSluys/ConstellationLines) dataset
by Marc van der Sluys, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The visual treatment,
projection, placement, and animation are original to this portfolio.

The twelve displayed patterns are Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo,
Libra, Scorpius, Sagittarius, Capricornus, Aquarius, and Pisces.
